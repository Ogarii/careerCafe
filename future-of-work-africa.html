Rethinking Africa's Future of Work
The initiative

Shaping work around Africa's people, priorities and potential.

Artificial intelligence, automation, changing labour markets and demographic transitions are reshaping careers, organisations and economies. Africa has an opportunity to do more than prepare for a future designed elsewhere.

The Future of Work in Africa Initiative brings high-level dialogue, research, strategic foresight, professional development and partnerships into one practical platform.

Our aim is to move the Future of Work conversation from discussion to practical, Africa-led action.
Our journey so far

Each stage builds on the one before it.

From continental convenings to individual career reflection and practical learning, the initiative has become a connected progression rather than a series of isolated events.

Nairobi | 4 June 2026

Where the conversation began

Leaders from government, business, academia, development institutions, training institutions and civil society examined the forces reshaping Africa's workforce and the case for active African leadership.

View Nairobi gallery
Kigali | 25 June 2026

Building a Pan-African agenda

In Kigali, the conversation moved from identifying challenges to the partnerships, evidence and foresight needed for sustained action. Together, Nairobi and Kigali established the foundation for the proposed Africa Future of Work Think Tank.

Career reflection in an uncertain world

In collaboration with Global Health Otherwise, a special Career Conversations webinar brought the agenda closer to lived professional experience: non-linear careers, adaptability, human skills, networks, AI and meaningful work.

Kigali | 7 September 2026

From knowledge to action

What we are learning

Africa's future workforce cannot be built using yesterday's assumptions.

Across the conversations, the same priorities keep returning: a stronger link between education and employment, labour-market intelligence, continuous reskilling, responsible AI adoption, human capabilities and deeper collaboration.

Education

Aligning learning pathways with the skills and realities of work.

Artificial Intelligence

Adopting technology responsibly while keeping people and value in view.

Health

Building resilient workforces and systems that support wellbeing.

Agriculture

Recognising the changing skills and opportunities within vital sectors.

Entrepreneurship

Creating the conditions for new forms of enterprise and livelihoods.

Strategic Foresight

Anticipating change instead of simply reacting to it.

Professional development

Turning insight into practical capability.

Identifying, creating and nurturing value in the future of work.

At the Future of Work Knowledge-to-Action Forum and Masterclass, participants worked on how to identify their value and transferable skills, communicate expertise with clarity, strengthen their positioning and build purposeful professional relationships.

The horizon

From convenings to a Pan-African platform.

Isolated events and periodic conversations will not be enough. The proposed Africa Future of Work Think Tank is an Africa-led platform envisioned to connect governments, the private sector, academia, development partners, civil society and young leaders around the changing world of work.

Its role is to anticipate workforce challenges and opportunities, strengthen labour-market intelligence and turn timely evidence into practical recommendations.

  • Anticipate workforce trends
  • Generate useful evidence
  • Strengthen strategic foresight
  • Translate knowledge into action
