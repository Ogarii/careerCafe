About Career Café

Building Africa's fit-for-purpose workforce for the future.

Career Café Africa is a Pan-African platform advancing career development, leadership, workforce transformation, and the future of work. We support individuals, employers, institutions, and policymakers across the career life course through mentorship, practical learning, workplace wellness, research, foresight, convening, and partnerships.

With hubs in Nairobi, Kigali, and Accra, we are building a continental ecosystem that aligns skills, education, leadership, technology, and labour-market needs to help Africa develop a fit-for-purpose, adaptable, and future-ready workforce.

01

Our mission

To build a fit-for-purpose and future-ready African workforce by connecting people, evidence, skills, leadership, institutions, and opportunities.

02

Our vision

An Africa where every generation is equipped, connected, and empowered to thrive in—and help shape—the future of work.
